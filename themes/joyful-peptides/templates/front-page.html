05 — QUESTIONS

Common questions

What does “research use only” mean?

All products sold on this site are intended strictly for laboratory and research use. They are not for human consumption, and are not sold, marketed, or intended for any diagnostic or therapeutic purpose.

Why do I need an account to order?

Every order requires a research account and a signed research-use attestation. This is how we record that each order was placed for in vitro research use by an eligible buyer.

How do I check a certificate of analysis?

Find the batch number printed on the vial label, then enter it in our COA library to see the independent lab's full report for that exact batch.
